Purity Protocol

Engineered for Safety, Balanced for Taste

Every bottle of Zippyfeed Kendrapara Packaged Drinking Water undergoes a strict 5-stage purification and quality inspection pipeline.

1. Micron Sand & Carbon Filtration

Removes turbidity, suspended particulate matter, physical sediments, and organic impurities, leaving base raw water visibly crystal-clear.

2. Reverse Osmosis (RO) Demineralization

High-pressure semi-permeable membranes strip out excess heavy dissolved solids, nitrates, and microscopic chemical compounds down to 0.0001 microns.

3. Precision Mineral Fortification

Purified water is carefully balanced with food-grade Sodium Bicarbonate (0.5mg/100ml), Magnesium Sulphate (0.2mg/100ml), and Potassium Bicarbonate (0.1mg/100ml) to achieve crisp refreshment and optimum pH.

4. Ultraviolet (UV) & Ozonation Barrier

Twin disinfection mechanisms destroy microbial bacteria and pathogens while enriching residual dissolved oxygen for long-lasting freshness inside sealed PET containers.

5. Automated Bottle Rinsing & Touchless Capping

Pre-forms are blown, immediately sanitized with sterile water rinses, filled, and hermetically capped without manual human contact.

Microbial Lab Testing

Each production batch is quarantined until internal laboratory microbial plating confirms zero coliform and compliance with IS 14543 standards.

Natural Mineral Balance

Why Added Minerals Make a Difference

Unlike distilled water which can taste flat and acidic, Zippyfeed provides essential electrolytes that replenish your daily hydration needs.

  • Sodium Bicarbonate: Maintains alkalinity and neutralizes palate acidity.
  • Magnesium Sulphate: Supports muscle relaxation and cellular hydration.
  • Potassium Bicarbonate: Essential intracellular electrolyte for vital energy.
