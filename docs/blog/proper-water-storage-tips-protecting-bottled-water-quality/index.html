How to Store Packaged Drinking Water to Preserve Freshness & Safety

Best practices for keeping case packs clean, cool, and safe from heat, sunlight, and humidity in residential and commercial pantries.

Packaged drinking water is sealed at our hygienic bottling plant under stringent microbial controls. However, how cases are stored in your home, warehouse, or retail shop plays a key role in preserving taste and packaging integrity over its shelf life.

Follow these manufacturer-recommended guidelines:

1. Keep Away from Direct Sunlight: Exposure to direct sun and UV radiation can cause photochemical stress on PET plastic and raise water temperature. Store cartons in a shaded pantry, cupboard, or temperature-controlled storage room.

2. Elevate from Concrete Floors: Keep stacked cartons slightly elevated on wooden pallets or plastic shelving rather than directly against damp concrete floors, especially during the monsoon season.

3. Avoid Storage Near Strong Chemicals or Fuels: PET plastic is a semi-permeable membrane. Never store water cases beside kerosene, paint thinners, chemical detergents, or pesticides, as volatile vapors can migrate through packaging over extended periods.

4. Practice First-In, First-Out (FIFO): When reordering cases for your restaurant or office pantry, arrange new crates at the back so older cartons are naturally consumed first.
